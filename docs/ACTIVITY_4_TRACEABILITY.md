## Safe rollback demonstration procedure

1. Use a disposable PostgreSQL database, never the shared/submission database.
2. Prepare one student and at least two available equipment items.
3. Trigger an intentional constraint failure after at least one borrowing insert has been staged in the multi-item transaction (for example, use a test-only seam or controlled unique-index conflict).
4. Confirm the service reports failure and does not report success.
5. Read Borrowings and Equipment through a **new context/connection**. Compare against the captured before-test results and confirm no partial borrowing rows and no partially unavailable equipment remain.
6. Record the deliberate failure point, expected outcome, observed outcome, and SQL/readback evidence.

Do not simulate commit-time connection loss and then claim rollback. Under BR-18, a lost commit acknowledgement can mean an unknown outcome; reconnect and inspect state before taking another action.
